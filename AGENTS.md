# Project Context & Instructions

This application is the **IHT Admission Academy** platform, focusing on providing resources for aspirants of the **Institute of Health Technology (IHT), Dhaka** (Mohakhali IHT) and other medical technology institutes in Bangladesh.

## Core Identity
- **Primary Institute:** Institute of Health Technology (IHT), Dhaka.
- **Principal:** Dr. Md. Mojib Uddin (MBBS, M.Phil).
- **Established:** 1963 (Oldest and most prestigious government medical technology institute in Bangladesh).
- **Location:** Mohakhali, Dhaka-1212.
- **Affiliations:** University of Dhaka (B.Sc.), State Medical Faculty (Diploma).

## Academic Programs Information
### 1. B.Sc. in Health Technology (4-Year Course)
Affiliated with the Faculty of Medicine, University of Dhaka:
- B.Sc. in Health Technology (Laboratory)
- B.Sc. in Radiology & Imaging
- B.Sc. in Physiotherapy

### 2. Diploma Courses (3-Year Course + 1-Year Internship)
Under the State Medical Faculty of Bangladesh:
- Laboratory Medicine
- Radiology & Imaging
- Physiotherapy
- Dentistry
- Pharmacy
- Sanitary Inspection (SIT)
- Radiotherapy
- Operation Theatre Assistant (OTA)
- Intensive Care Assistant (ICA)
